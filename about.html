About Me

I am Karen Barboza, a multi-disciplinary Art Director and Designer focused on creating tactile typographic systems, editorial design, and engaging visual stories.

Through structured layout frameworks, high-contrast monochrome experiments, and rich visual textures, I bridge the gap between digital interactivity and print aesthetics. My practice operates at the intersection of creative direction, packaging, and brand strategy.

Capabilities

• Art Direction
• Editorial & Print Design
• Branding & Visual Identity
• Motion Graphics
• Packaging Design
• UI/UX Design
